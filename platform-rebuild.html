Rebuild the platform without stopping the business

Old platforms collect features nobody uses and data nobody wants to touch. We decide with you what the rebuild keeps, what it drops and how data moves, then release in planned stages so operations keep running.

Last updated: 2026-11-06

What a rebuild covers

  • Review of current features, users and dependencies
  • A first-release scope separated from later improvements
  • A staged data migration plan with validation
  • Load and acceptance criteria defined before launch
  • Operating handover and launch checks

Why rebuilds overrun

Teams try to carry every existing feature into the new version, and data migration is left until the end. We review real usage first, agree a smaller first release, and plan migration in stages from the start, with an owner for each part.

About Decisionary

Decisionary is a custom software development company in Seoul, South Korea. It works with businesses and digital agencies on web applications, platform rebuilds and API integrations. Scope and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing before the build, and the founder leads delivery.

FAQ

Do we have to move everything at once?

No. We plan the rebuild and data migration in stages, so the current platform keeps running until each part is ready.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. Responsibilities between your team, our specialists and Decisionary are agreed before the project starts.
