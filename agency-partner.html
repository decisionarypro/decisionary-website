How white-label work runs

  • We deliver under your agency's name; your client sees your team
  • Keep the client relationship yourself, or hand day-to-day contact to us
  • Communication roles, confidentiality and commercial terms are agreed per project

Before you quote your client

Agencies usually get burned at the quote: a timeline is promised before anyone has looked at integrations and edge cases. Send us the brief first. We review it with you, flag what is unclear, and help you set a timeline and acceptance criteria you can stand behind.

About Decisionary

Decisionary is a custom software development company in Seoul, South Korea. It works with businesses and digital agencies on web applications, platform rebuilds and API integrations. Scope and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing before the build, and the founder leads delivery.

FAQ

Will you contact our client directly?

Only if you ask us to. Client contact, communication roles and confidentiality are agreed before the project starts.

Can you help before we win the project?

Yes. We can review the brief with you and help shape the scope and estimate for your proposal.

How do you price agency work?

After reviewing deliverables, dependencies and acceptance criteria. Fixed-price contracts are available, and we can also invoice by milestone.
