05 / Common questions

Before we
start.

Does Decisionary use an in-house development team?

Decisionary operates as a project delivery business. Qualified independent PM, engineering, design and QA specialists are selected and confirmed for each engagement. We do not represent independent specialists as permanent employees.

Which technologies and platforms do you support?

Our service areas are defined by the business outcomes above. Technical stacks, delivery resources and integrations are evaluated against the brief before we confirm capability, schedule and pricing.

How do you price projects?

We scope projects around expected deliverables and milestones. Estimates are prepared after reviewing requirements and confirming specialist capacity. We do not quote an unverified standard hourly rate.

Can you work with our digital agency?

Yes. We welcome delivery partnership discussions, including white-label arrangements, when contract ownership, confidentiality, scope and working methods can be agreed in advance.
