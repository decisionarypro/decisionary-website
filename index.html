06 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

What types of projects are a fit?

Business web applications, platform rebuilds, and API or data integrations with a definable scope. We review the brief first to establish whether a suitable team and delivery plan can be confirmed.

Does Decisionary have a permanent development team?

We use a project-based delivery model. Independent engineering, PM, design, and QA specialists are selected and confirmed for the scope of each engagement; they are not presented as permanent employees.

How do you calculate project pricing?

We estimate after reviewing deliverables, dependencies, acceptance requirements, and specialist capacity. A fixed price or milestone-based structure may be proposed when the scope supports it.

Can you work as our agency's development partner?

Yes. We welcome white-label and agency-led collaboration discussions. Client ownership, confidentiality, delivery responsibilities, and communication boundaries are agreed in advance.

Can you support Korea and East Asia projects?

We are based in South Korea and can explore local coordination where market knowledge and software delivery requirements overlap. Project suitability is evaluated individually.
