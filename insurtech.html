What we build

  • Online sign-up and application flows with fewer steps and fields
  • Partner and embedded insurance channels through APIs
  • Admin tools for products, contracts and partner settings
  • Payment, settlement and exception handling across the sign-up journey
  • Data migration and integration with existing core systems

Where insurance projects usually go wrong

Each partner wants its own fields and rules, operations depend on manual exceptions, and the customer screens get redesigned while the product rules underneath stay unclear. Scope then keeps moving. We map product rules, partner requirements and operational exceptions first, and only then agree screens and acceptance criteria.

FAQ

Do you work with insurers or with insurtech startups?

Both. We work with insurers, general agencies and insurtech teams, and with digital agencies building insurance products for their clients.

Can you work with our existing core insurance system?

Yes, through its APIs or data interfaces. We review what the core system exposes before agreeing scope, and plan any data migration in stages.

Can we start with a small scope?

Yes. A one to two week scope definition gives you requirements, a screen and feature list, acceptance criteria and a fixed estimate. If you go ahead with the build, the fee is credited in full.
