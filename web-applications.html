How we keep the first release small

Most web projects grow because every request goes into version one. We look at how the work is done today, sort features into what the first release needs and what can wait, and write that down with acceptance criteria before development starts.

About Decisionary

Decisionary is a custom software development company in Seoul, South Korea. It works with businesses and digital agencies on web applications, platform rebuilds and API integrations. Scope and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing before the build, and the founder leads delivery.

FAQ

Which technologies do you use?

We choose the stack after reading your requirements and existing systems, and confirm it in the statement of work.

Who owns the code?

Ownership and handover of code and documents are agreed in the contract before the build starts.

Can you maintain it after launch?

Operating handover is planned before the build. Ongoing work after launch is agreed separately.
