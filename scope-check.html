Before you outsource,
check your scope first

Answer 10 questions to see how likely your scope is to change mid-build, and the 3 things to settle before you ask for a quote.

Results right away, no sign-up · We don’t ask for your name or contact details

What the check asks

Ten questions in five areas. Each answer is scored 0 (settled), 1 (partly) or 2 (open), and the total becomes a 0–100 scope risk score. Lower is better.

Scope

  1. Is the list of features you want written down?
  2. Have you separated what the first release needs from what can wait?

Acceptance

  1. Does each feature have a “done when…” condition?

Integration & data

  1. How many external systems need to be connected?
  2. Do those systems have API documentation and a test environment?
  3. Do you need to move data from an existing service?

Business rules

  1. Are exception rules for payments, settlement and permissions written down?

Decisions & timeline

  1. Who makes the final call on requirements?
  2. How was the launch date set?
  3. Is there a budget range?
