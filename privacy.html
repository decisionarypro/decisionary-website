Privacy information

Last reviewed: 23 October 2026

This website is operated for business enquiries by Decisionary, an independent business based in South Korea.

What we receive

This website does not have an on-site enquiry form or client account. When you click an email link, your own mail app handles the message. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, business contact details and project summary.

How we use that information

We use enquiries to evaluate potential work, reply to you, and prepare discussions or proposals. We do not sell enquiry details.

Website storage and technical data

This site uses browser local storage only to remember a chosen display language. We do not intentionally install third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts in this version. A website host may process ordinary server access logs under its own infrastructure and privacy terms.

Retention and requests

Business correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary for follow-up, administration, legal obligations or dispute resolution. To ask about access, correction or deletion, email hello@decisionarypro.com; applicable legal retention duties may limit deletion.

International communications

Because we are based in South Korea, information shared by overseas enquirers may be accessed and handled in South Korea and by their chosen email providers.
